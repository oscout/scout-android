# Scout documentation illustration

Conceptual artwork identifying the integration, not a product screenshot or an endorsement.

The light and dark illustrations share the same geometry. Their platform mark is embedded, so the SVG needs no network requests or sibling image files. Each asset includes an accessible title and description.

## Mark provenance

- Scout: canonical outlined hexagon and filled center from the Scout brand kit.
- Android: Android robot head from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/?q=android) 13.21.0 (CC0 vector of Google's mark), drawn in theme ink. Android is a trademark of Google LLC.

Platform marks and names remain the property of their respective owners. The original mark geometry is retained.
